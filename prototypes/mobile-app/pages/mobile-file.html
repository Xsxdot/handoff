handoff Hand an implementation plan to another AI. You just review. handoff is a CLI-only, two-role collaboration tool. You — or any coding-agent session: Claude Code, opencode, grok, and codex all work — play the coordinator: write the plan, dispatch the task, rule on permissions, review the changes. The executor does the actual work in its own independent session. write plan → handoff dispatch → executor works independently ↑ │ reply: approve/answer ←── permission gate / question wakes you Why not just open a terminal and let the AI run? · Execution is separated from review… · Nothing is lost when you disconnect… · Remote compute… · No central anything…
